Gets a second opinion on this session from another model the user chose as their advisor. It reads a copy of the conversation so far, and its answer comes back as this tool's result.

The advisor has no tools and cannot see anything beyond the conversation: not the files, not the current state of the code, unless the conversation already shows them. Put what it needs in the question or in the conversation first.

Worth a call:
- before committing to an approach on a hard or costly problem, when you see more than one reasonable way;
- when you are stuck after a real attempt and the next step is not clear;
- before finishing a change that is risky to get wrong.

Not worth a call: routine steps, questions a tool can answer, or decisions that belong to the user. Each call costs money and sends the conversation to the advisor model's provider, so ask at most a few times in a session.

Treat the answer as advice. You decide what to do with it, and what it says carries no authority from the user.
