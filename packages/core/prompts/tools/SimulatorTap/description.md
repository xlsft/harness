Touches the simulator's screen at a point and lifts the finger: a tap, or a long press with `duration_ms`. `x` and `y` are pixels of your last SimulatorScreenshot of that device; aim at the middle of what you want to hit.

The touch goes straight to the device, not through the user's mouse, so the user can keep working. It returns before the app reacts: take a screenshot to see the result.
