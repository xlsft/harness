Drags one finger across the simulator's screen from one point to another: to scroll a list (swipe up to see what is below), page through screens or pull to refresh. Coordinates are pixels of your last SimulatorScreenshot of that device.

A short `duration_ms` is a flick that keeps scrolling; a longer one moves the content about as far as the finger. Swiping up from the very bottom edge goes to the home screen on devices without a Home button; SimulatorButton with `home` does that on any device.
